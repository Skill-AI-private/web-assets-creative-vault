# Playbook 04: Developer Design Resources Cheat Sheet

Synthesized from **`bradtraversy/design-resources-for-developers`** and **`noobnooc/awesome-design-dev`**.

---

## 1. High-Resolution Stock Imagery (Free & Commercial License)
- **Unsplash:** [unsplash.com](https://unsplash.com) — Modern photography, generous API.
- **Pexels:** [pexels.com](https://pexels.com) — High quality photography and stock video clips.

## 2. Color Palette Generators
- **Coolors:** [coolors.co](https://coolors.co) — Rapid accessible palette generation.
- **Realtime Colors:** [realtimecolors.com](https://realtimecolors.com) — Test color systems directly on live UI components.

## 3. Image Optimization & SVG Cleanup
- **SVGOMG:** [jakearchibald.github.io/svgomg](https://jakearchibald.github.io/svgomg/) — Strip redundant XML tags from SVGs before embedding.
- **Squoosh:** [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) — Convert PNGs/JPEGs to modern WebP / AVIF formats.
